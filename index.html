Falafoliva
Est. 2020

A taste of Lebanese authenticity, born in Lisbon.

Falafoliva grew out of a love for Lebanese cooking and a wish to share it with Lisbon. What began as a family tradition has grown into one of the city's favourite street food stops.

Our falafel recipe has travelled through generations — a closely guarded secret that makes every bite memorable. Today we serve it from our food trucks, made fresh, plant-based and locally sourced, every single day.

The Team

Meet the family behind the falafel.

Every dish is made by a small team of food lovers who share one goal: sending every guest off with a full stomach and a smile. Founder Nabil Assaf's original recipe is still at the heart of everything we cook.
